# Consigne commune : dessiner un héros HD (pixel art professionnel, ~96 px)

You are a top-tier professional pixel artist (the best mobile RPG sprite artists). Draw ONE hero sprite, by hand, pixel by pixel.

CONTEXT
- Project: mobile hero game "Fuze Arena – Hero Battle" (/home/user/fuze-arena-hero-battle). The owner (French, not a developer) found the old pixel heroes ugly (same torso and same head/face for everyone, heavy blobby limbs, round-blob hands, simple shapes stacked, not detailed, not real 3/4). He wants each hero "100 times more beautiful and detailed", professional pixel art, true 3/4 stance facing right, its own body shape and its own head (real animal anatomy for creatures), coherent, different, badass.
- THE QUALITY BAR: many heroes are already done and VALIDATED by the owner ("vraiment top, on garde exactement le même style et la même précision"): refonte-graphique/pixel/hd/kesha.txt, elyon.txt, cyclope.txt, ilian.txt, zaroth.txt, velith.txt, barzul.txt, lilou.txt, celia.txt, nerith.txt, meduse.txt, arachne.txt, solenn.txt, auriel.txt, gabryel.txt, kael.txt, rook.txt, isis.txt, sable.txt, tybalt.txt, berold.txt, aldric.txt, ysolde.txt. Study at least three of them closely before drawing (`python3 hd.py voir kesha`, `voir elyon`, `voir cyclope`, `voir ilian`…, then look at sortie/<name>-net.png and -grand.png; read their files: palettes, notes, how hands, heads, shading, details are done). Your hero must match EXACTLY the same style, scale, outline, lighting (single light from top-right), hue-shifted ramps, precision and detail density — while having its own body type, head and identity.
- Rules: refonte-graphique/pixel/hd/GUIDE.md (read fully; it overrides the older refonte-graphique/pixel/GUIDE.md "inked" style). File format and tools: header of refonte-graphique/pixel/hd/hd.py.
- Owner feedback already given on the validated ones (avoid these): a strap/baldric must follow the torso form naturally (one clean diagonal, not odd vertical straps); no black inside mouths (dark red throat instead); the far eye in 3/4 must have the same shape language as the near eye, on the same eye line, just a bit narrower.
- Tools (from refonte-graphique/pixel/hd): `python3 hd.py voir <name>` (sortie/<name>-grand.png ×8 with ruler in GRID coordinates, -net.png ×6, -jeu.png in-game size, -anim.png 8 frames), `python3 hd.py zone <name> x0 y0 x1 y1`, `python3 hd.py pose <name> x y TEXT`, `python3 hd.py controle <name>`. You may view as many images as needed and crop/zoom with small PIL scripts. You may bootstrap big masses with a small Python script, but the final must be refined pixel by pixel.
- Original painted concept: public/art/p/<id>.webp. Old rejected pixel sheet: public/art/px/<id>.webp (8 frames ×3). Theme notes: head comment of refonte-graphique/pixel/persos/<name>.py. Keep the theme, main colors and weapon; improve everything else.
- `sol` is placed at (64,116) of a 128×120 frame: the drawing may extend from sol.x−62 to sol.x+61 and up to sol.y−114; feet flat on the `sol` row. Height ground→top of head ≈ 96 px.
- Put your scratch scripts and images ONLY in your own folder <BROUILLON>/<name>/ (other artists work at the same time).
- Notes inside the file (# lines) in French. Do NOT modify any other file of the repo, do not run jeu.py, do not commit, do not push.

TASK
1. Read the guide, the hd.py header, study the 3 validated heroes, look at the concept, the old sheet and the old notes.
2. Write French notes at the top of refonte-graphique/pixel/hd/<name>.txt (body type, 3/4 combat stance, head design, signature details, palette plan), then draw it (id, sol, tete, souffle, cligne with eye-only letters, palette, calques; a fixed calque for a weapon planted on the ground if needed).
3. Self-review at least 4 passes: voir → look at -grand.png AND -jeu.png and zoomed crops of head, hands, feet, weapon → compare side by side with the validated heroes → fix. `python3 hd.py controle <name>` must end with zero FAUTE.
4. Final answer (plain text, short, in French): what you drew, the main choices, and anything the owner should check.
